# Privacy Policy — Anomalous Devs | APP

**Last Updated:** August 27, 2026

Anomalous Devs | APP (“the Bot”) is a Discord bot designed for use in SCP:RP building and development communities. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot may collect, how that information is used, and how it is protected.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled in a server, the Bot may process limited Discord-related information, including:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord server IDs
* Discord channel IDs
* Discord role IDs
* Discord usernames and display names
* Messages or commands sent to the Bot
* Moderation-related information, such as warnings or other server actions

The Bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, home addresses, or government identification.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

* Execute commands and provide requested features
* Provide moderation and administrative tools
* Maintain server logs when logging features are enabled
* Manage permissions and roles
* Provide development and building-related utilities
* Prevent abuse or misuse of the Bot
* Maintain and improve the Bot's functionality

The Bot does not sell user information or use collected information for advertising.

## 3. Message Content

The Bot may process message content when required for a specific command or feature. Message content is not intentionally stored permanently unless a server feature specifically requires it, such as moderation or logging.

## 4. Data Storage

Information required for the Bot's features may be stored temporarily or persistently depending on the feature being used.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring logging and other features within their servers. Information stored by these features may remain until it is deleted by the Bot's administrators or the relevant server configuration is changed.

## 5. Data Sharing

Anomalous Devs | APP does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may only be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

* Operate the Bot and its services
* Protect the Bot or its users from abuse
* Comply with applicable laws or legal requests

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid sending passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information through the Bot.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the Bot's features and operation.

If you would like information associated with your Discord account to be deleted, you may contact the Bot's developers or server administrators.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate administrative, security, or legal purposes.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with Discord's services and APIs. Discord's own policies and terms may also apply when using the Bot.

The Bot may also use third-party services necessary for hosting or providing specific features.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Anomalous Devs | APP is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children outside of what is necessary for the Bot to function through Discord.

Because the Bot operates within Discord, users should also review Discord's applicable Terms of Service and privacy policies.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the Bot's features or data practices change.

Any significant changes may be reflected by updating the “Last Updated” date on this page.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or information processed by Anomalous Devs | APP, please contact the developers through the official Anomalous Devs | APP Discord server.
